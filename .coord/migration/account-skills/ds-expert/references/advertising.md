# Advertising & Customer Acquisition

Read this before advising on ads, creative, or scaling spend. Tie every
recommendation back to the user's budget and breakeven ROAS
(`pricing-and-unit-economics.md`).

## Platform roles — they are not interchangeable

> **Meta closes. TikTok discovers.**

- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** excels at converting people with existing
  intent — site visitors, engagers, add-to-carts. Its retargeting and broad
  prospecting (in 2026, **broad targeting + strong creative beats narrow
  interest stacking** — the algorithm finds buyers better than manual targeting)
  make it the workhorse for conversion.
- **TikTok** is a discovery engine. The algorithm distributes content on viewer
  behavior (watch time, engagement velocity) more than advertiser targeting, so
  **creative *is* your targeting.** Native, story-driven, UGC-style content
  beats polished ads. Whoever tests the most creatives wins — brands testing
  100+ videos/month beat brands testing 10.
- **Google (Search/Shopping/PMax)** captures existing demand — best when there's
  real search volume for the product/problem. Lower volume but high intent;
  great complement once a product proves out.

Most stores start on **one** platform (usually the one matching the product:
visual/impulse → TikTok or Meta; intent/search → Google), prove the product,
then add a second.

## Budgets and testing structure

- First month: ~**$600–$1,500** across 1–2 platforms; TikTok wants
  **$1,000–$3,000/month** minimum to test properly.
- Run campaigns in **three phases:**
  1. **Testing** — small budgets across multiple creatives/angles to find
     winners. Kill losers fast; keep what beats breakeven.
  2. **Scaling** — increase budget on proven creatives/audiences (duplicate or
     raise budget gradually to avoid resetting learning).
  3. **Consolidation** — combine the winning elements (offer, hook, audience)
     into stable, efficient campaigns.

## Benchmarks to judge performance

- **CTR:** ~1–3% cold audiences, ~3–8% retargeting.
- **Breakeven ROAS:** ~2–3× at typical margins (compute the exact number from
  the unit economics).
- **Scaling threshold:** ~3–5× ROAS before increasing spend.
- **TikTok ROAS:** ~2:1 to break even at typical margins, aim for 3:1+.
- Always judge on **true/blended ROAS** net of fees, refunds, and VAT — use
  TrueProfit or Triple Whale, not just the ad platform's reported ROAS.

## Creative — the real lever

- Creative is where wins are made, especially on TikTok. Prioritize **volume and
  variety of hooks/angles** over a few "perfect" ads.
- Favor **UGC and native-feeling video**; show the product solving a problem in
  the first 1–3 seconds.
- Tools (categories then specifics): UGC creator marketplaces (Billo, TikTok
  Creator Marketplace), AI ad/creative tools (Creatify, AdCreative), ad-spy for
  inspiration (Winning Hunter, Minea, TikTok Creative Center), analytics
  (Cometly, Triple Whale, TrueProfit).

## Owned channels (don't skip these)

Paid traffic is rented; owned channels compound:

- **Email/SMS via Klaviyo** — abandoned-cart, post-purchase, and win-back flows
  recover revenue cheaply. SMS works well in Israel.
- **Organic TikTok/Instagram/Reels** — posting native content builds free reach
  and doubles as creative testing. A viable low-budget starting point.
- **Retargeting** — always have retargeting live; it's the highest-ROAS spend.

## Israel-specific advertising notes

- Run creative in **Hebrew** for the Israeli audience (and RTL landing pages);
  localized creative and offers convert far better than translated English.
- Watch **CPMs and seasonality** around Israeli holidays.
- Influencer marketing is strong in the Israeli market — local micro-influencers
  can outperform cold paid traffic for trust.
- Ensure ad claims and the store comply with Israeli consumer law (honest
  pricing incl. VAT, clear cancellation terms) — see `israel-market.md`. Meta
  and TikTok also restrict certain product categories.
