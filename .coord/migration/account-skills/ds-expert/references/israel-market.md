# Israeli Market: Payments, Tax, Law, Language

Read this before advising on anything Israel-specific: payment setup, pricing
presentation, store policies, language, or shipping into Israel. This is where
generic dropshipping advice goes wrong — Israel has its own gateways, VAT rules,
and consumer law.

> Not legal or tax advice. For business registration, VAT filing, and policy
> wording, the user should confirm with an Israeli accountant (רו"ח) or lawyer.

## Payment gateways

Shopify Payments is not the default route for Israeli merchants; most use a local
clearing house ("סליקה"). Common options:

- **Tranzila** — now an official Shopify payment gateway in Israel and worldwide;
  Hebrew checkout and local payment methods.
- **Cardcom**, **PayPlus**, **Pelecard**, **Meshulam / Grow** — widely used local
  clearing houses. Fees typically range ~**1.5%–3.5%**.
- Add **PayPal**, **Apple Pay**, **Google Pay** (and credit cards) for
  international customers.
- Offer **payments / installments (תשלומים)** — Israeli shoppers expect the
  option to split credit-card payments; it lifts conversion and AOV.

Choose based on: fees, Shopify integration quality, installment support, payout
timing, and whether they support the user's business entity.

## VAT (מע"מ)

- Israel's VAT is **18%** on most goods/services.
- For B2C, present prices **VAT-inclusive** and clearly; B2B audiences may expect
  ex-VAT presentation. Correct presentation for the target audience is a legal
  compliance point.
- The business **collects and remits** VAT — model it in pricing
  (`pricing-and-unit-economics.md`) so it isn't silently eating margin.
- Importing goods into Israel can trigger **import VAT and customs**; factor into
  landed cost and delivery promises.

## Business registration

- Israeli sellers typically operate as **עוסק פטור** (below the annual turnover
  threshold, simplified, can't charge/reclaim VAT), **עוסק מורשה** (charges and
  reports VAT — the usual choice once selling seriously), or **חברה בע"מ**
  (limited company). The right structure depends on turnover and liability —
  refer the user to an accountant, but make sure they know they need to register
  before selling.

## Consumer Protection Law (חוק הגנת הצרכן) — distance selling

Online sales are "distance-selling transactions" with specific obligations:

- **14-day cancellation right:** consumers may cancel within **14 days** of the
  transaction or of receiving the goods/transaction summary (special categories
  differ). The store must disclose cancellation terms.
- **Refund timeline:** on cancellation, the seller must refund within **7 days**
  of receiving the cancellation notice.
- **Return condition:** item generally must be undamaged/unused; original
  packaging is treated as proof it's unused.
- **Required disclosures:** clear cancellation method and terms, full price incl.
  VAT, supplier identity, and delivery details must be presented before purchase.

Practical implication: the store needs proper Hebrew **Terms / Returns /
Cancellation** pages reflecting these rights. Generic imported Shopify policy
templates usually don't comply — adapt them.

## Language & UX (Hebrew / RTL)

- A store targeting Israelis should be **Hebrew and right-to-left (RTL)**. Pick a
  theme that handles RTL cleanly (layout, alignment, fonts) — many dropshipping
  themes are LTR-first and look broken in Hebrew.
- Localize everything: product copy, currency in **₪ (ILS)**, checkout, emails,
  ad creative, and support — not machine-translated English.
- Consider **bilingual** (Hebrew + English) only if genuinely selling abroad too;
  otherwise focus beats breadth.

## Shipping & fulfillment in Israel

- Domestic delivery expectations are fast; a **local warehouse/3PL** for hero
  products is a strong differentiator (see `suppliers-and-fulfillment.md`).
- Direct China→Israel parcels may face customs/standards holds for regulated
  categories (electronics, cosmetics, toys, supplements, electrical). Verify the
  product can be imported and set honest delivery windows.
- Offer recognizable local delivery/pickup options where possible.
