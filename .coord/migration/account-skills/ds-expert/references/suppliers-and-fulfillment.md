# Suppliers & Fulfillment

Read this before advising on sourcing, suppliers, or shipping times.

## The core trade-off

Every sourcing decision sits on a line between **cheap-and-slow** and
**branded-and-fast**:

- **AliExpress / open marketplaces** — lowest cost, no minimums, huge selection,
  but 2–4 week shipping, generic packaging, inconsistent quality, and thin
  margins (often 2–22% on classic Chinese dropshipping before your time).
- **Vetted networks (Zendrop, Spocket, CJ)** — curated suppliers, faster
  shipping (some US/EU warehouses → 3–8 days), branding/custom packaging
  options, higher cost.
- **Private sourcing agent** — once a product proves out, a dedicated agent
  negotiates price, handles QC, offers custom branding and faster lines. Best
  margins and control at volume; needs order flow to be worth it.
- **Local (Israeli) warehouse / supplier** — fastest domestic delivery and
  easiest returns for an Israel-focused store; higher unit cost, smaller
  selection. Strong differentiator in-market.

In 2026, buyers expect 3–7 day delivery with transparent tracking. Long generic
shipping is one of the biggest causes of refunds, chargebacks, and bad reviews.
Push users toward faster fulfillment as soon as a product is validated, even if
they start on AliExpress for testing.

## Supplier vetting checklist (always order samples)

1. Check ratings, reviews, and response time before committing.
2. **Order a sample** — test real quality, real shipping time, and packaging.
3. Start with **small test orders** before moving a supplier to a hero product.
4. See how they communicate when something goes wrong (delays, defects).
5. Confirm capacity and restock reliability.
6. **Keep 2–3 vetted suppliers per hero product** — single-source dependency is
   a major risk and kills negotiating leverage.

## Apps / platforms (categories then specifics)

- **AliExpress automation:** DSers (the standard for AliExpress sourcing and
  order processing).
- **Vetted/branded, faster shipping:** Zendrop (private network of 30k+ vetted
  manufacturers, US + China, branding/custom packaging), Spocket (strong for
  US/EU suppliers and fast Western shipping).
- **Broad automation across sources:** AutoDS (imports, price/stock sync, order
  processing, tracking; transparent pricing, no order limits).
- **Other:** CJ Dropshipping (sourcing + warehousing + fulfillment, free tier),
  Syncee and Doba (supplier marketplaces), Dropified.

Match the app to the stage: test on DSers/AliExpress → move winners to
Zendrop/Spocket or CJ for speed/branding → graduate to a private agent or local
warehouse at volume. See `shopify-apps.md` for the full app stack.

## Shipping into Israel

- Direct China→Israel parcels can be slow and may face **customs/VAT** handling
  and standards checks for certain categories. Factor this into the promised
  delivery window and set honest expectations on the product page.
- For an Israel-focused brand, a **local 3PL or warehouse** for hero products
  dramatically improves delivery time, returns handling, and trust.
- Always show realistic tracking and delivery estimates; under-promise.
