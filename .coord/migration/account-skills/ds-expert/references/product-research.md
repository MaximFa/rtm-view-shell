# Product & Niche Research

Read this before advising on what to sell or how to validate a product.

## The 2026 reality

The winning approach is **hyper-specialization**: pick a specific
micro-community with a real, recurring need and serve it with high-quality,
problem-solving products. Generic "trending products" stores are saturated and
hard to make profitable. The store that grows is the one with a coherent niche,
brand, and audience it understands.

## What makes a good dropshipping product

Favor products that are:

- **Lightweight and durable** — cheaper to ship, fewer breakages, lower returns.
- **Problem-solving or "wow" factor** — something that's easy to demonstrate in
  a short video and that the customer can't easily find in a local shop.
- **Healthy margin** — enough gross margin to absorb ad costs (see
  `pricing-and-unit-economics.md`). Aim for products you can sell at 2.5–3.5×
  landed cost.
- **Low return / low-regulation** — avoid electronics with warranty
  expectations, fragile goods, perishables, and anything with safety/standards
  regulation (cosmetics, supplements, kids' items, electrical) unless the user
  is ready for the compliance burden, which is heavier when importing into
  Israel.
- **Not trivially comparison-shopped** — if the exact item is one search away on
  a local marketplace at half the price, paid traffic won't be profitable.

## How to validate demand (use real data, not vanity metrics)

Push the user away from "this video has 2M views so it'll sell." Look at:

- **Units sold and revenue trends** over 7 / 30 / 90 days (TikTok product
  analytics, marketplace bestseller ranks).
- **Affiliate / creator count** on TikTok — many creators actively promoting a
  product (e.g. 50+) signals there's real money in it.
- **Ad longevity** — competitors running the same ad/creative for weeks usually
  means it's profitable.
- **Search demand** — Google Trends and keyword volume for intent-driven niches
  (especially for Google Shopping / search strategies).

- **Categories:** product-research/spy tools, marketplace bestseller lists,
  social trend trackers, keyword tools.
- **Named tools (2026):** TikTok Creative Center and TikTok product analytics,
  AutoDS marketplace and "winning products" hub, Spocket/Dropship.io product
  research, ZIK Analytics (strong for marketplace research), Winning Hunter /
  ad-spy tools for tracking competitor ads, Google Trends (free), Koala
  Inspector / similar for inspecting competitor Shopify stores.

## Store type decision

Help the user pick:

- **One-product store** — best for a single hero product with a strong angle;
  cleanest for paid traffic and branding; highest risk if the product dies.
- **Niche store** — a focused catalog around one audience; lets you cross-sell
  and raise AOV; the recommended default for most.
- **General store** — only as a fast testing ground to find a winner, then
  rebuild as a niche/one-product brand. Not a long-term brand.

## Israel-specific product notes

- Confirm the product clears Israeli **customs/standards** if imported directly
  to the customer (electronics, cosmetics, toys, and food/supplements can be
  held or require a standards import license). See `israel-market.md`.
- Consider **local sourcing or a local warehouse** for hero products to hit
  fast domestic delivery — a major competitive edge in a small market where word
  travels fast.
- Seasonality and holidays differ (e.g. demand spikes around Israeli holidays);
  align launches accordingly.
