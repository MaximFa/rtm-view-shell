# Shopify App Stack

Read this before recommending apps. Lead with the category and what it's for,
then name specific options. Don't over-install — every app is a monthly cost and
a potential speed/conflict issue. Recommend the minimum viable stack for the
user's stage.

## Why Shopify for this user

Shopify is the recommended base: best app ecosystem, clean checkout, native
support in Israel including local payment gateways (Tranzila is an official
Shopify payment gateway in Israel), multi-currency, and easy Hebrew/RTL themes.

## The minimum viable stack (beginner)

1. **Sourcing/automation** — one of: DSers (AliExpress), Zendrop or Spocket
   (faster/branded), AutoDS (broad automation). Pick based on the fulfillment
   decision in `suppliers-and-fulfillment.md`.
2. **Theme** — a fast, conversion-focused theme (Shopify's Dawn is free and
   solid; paid options like Shrine/Booster are common in dropshipping). Must
   support RTL cleanly for a Hebrew store.
3. **Reviews / social proof** — Loox or Judge.me (photo reviews build trust;
   essential for cold paid traffic).
4. **Payment gateway** — see `israel-market.md` (Tranzila, Cardcom, PayPlus,
   Meshulam/Grow, Pelecard) plus PayPal/Apple Pay/Google Pay for international.
5. **Tracking page** — 17TRACK or AfterShip so customers can self-serve tracking
   (cuts "where is my order?" tickets dramatically).

## Add as you grow

- **Upsell / AOV:** ReConvert, Zipify OneClickUpsell, or Shopify's native
  bundles — raise average order value to improve ad economics.
- **Email/SMS:** Klaviyo (the standard for e-commerce flows: abandoned cart,
  post-purchase, win-back). SMS is effective in Israel — consider a local SMS
  provider.
- **Profit/ad analytics:** TrueProfit or Triple Whale to see real net profit per
  order and true ROAS across ad platforms (critical once you're spending).
- **Customer service / helpdesk:** Gorgias or Shopify Inbox; a fast,
  consolidated inbox supports the ~2-hour response standard.
- **Reviews import / UGC:** tools to import supplier reviews ethically and
  collect customer photos/videos.

## Hygiene

- Audit apps quarterly; remove anything you're not using — they slow the store
  and add cost.
- Test page speed (Google PageSpeed / Shopify's speed report); slow mobile pages
  kill paid-traffic conversion.
- Keep checkout friction low: guest checkout, local payment methods, clear
  shipping and VAT presentation.
