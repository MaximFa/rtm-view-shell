# Hooks, Headlines & Awareness Stages

Read this when writing hooks or headlines. The hook is the highest-leverage part
of any ad — the average ad grabs only ~20% of viewers, so the hook decides reach
and cost.

## Hook anatomy (first 2–3 seconds, two parts)

**Part 1 — grab attention (0–1s).** The viewer can't yet read captions or hear
much; they react to what's *on screen*. Use:
- **Bold/contrasting visual** — `contrast` is the common trait of strong hooks
  (clashing colors, something unexpected, fast cuts, changing shots).
- **Audience callout** — "כל מי שטס לחו"ל חייב לדעת את זה."
- **Text as hook** — a headline on screen (use the 6 strategies below). The
  visual must match the text.
- **Show them themselves** (someone like them) or **their dream** (the result).

**Part 2 — open the loop (1–3s).** Give a reason to keep watching: a promise or
curiosity gap. Negative promise ("ככה תפסיק ל-X") or positive ("ככה תשיג X").
Don't resolve it — that's what keeps them watching into the body.

**Organic vs ad hooks:** organic content wants the *widest* attention; an ad hook
should grab the *right* viewer (your avatar) — the goal is money, not views. Bake
the avatar into the hook.

**Always produce 3 hook variants** per video, swappable as the first 3 seconds,
each a different angle of attack (problem callout / open-loop curiosity /
before-after contrast / audience callout). Format each as: visual · on-screen
text · spoken opening line.

## The 6 headline strategies

1. **Conclusion / curiosity (half story):** give half — they need the rest.
   "לא תאמינו מהו המאכל שגורם לכם להרגיש עייפים." Don't answer it in the headline.
2. **Benefit:** state the gain up front — for solution-aware / warm audiences.
   "איך לישון בטיסה בלי שהראש נופל."
3. **"How to":** direct promise of a solution to a specific problem.
   "איך להיפטר מכאבי גב בלי טיפולים יקרים."
4. **Problem-based:** lead with the problem, hint the solution — for
   problem-aware audiences (most common). "סובלים מקשיי הרדמות? הכירו את ה…".
5. **List:** "5 הסיבות ש…" — people want to know what's #1.
6. **Scarcity / urgency:** trigger FOMO. "24 שעות אחרונות", "נותרו יחידות בודדות."

There is no single best headline — **test**.

## Awareness-stage → hook/headline matrix

Match the message to where the customer's head is:

| Stage | What they know | Hook/headline approach | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unaware** | No problem in mind | Spark curiosity / make them feel the problem | "מרגיש עייף גם אחרי 8 שעות שינה?" |
| **Problem-aware** | Feels the problem, not the fix | Lead with the problem, hint the solution | "סובלים מיובש בעור? הנה הסוד…" |
| **Solution-aware** | Knows solutions exist | Focus on result + your mechanism | "ככה תרדמו תוך דקות עם הכרית הזו" |
| **Product-aware** | Knows your product | Differentiate; why yours is best | "זו הסיבה שכרית X היא הפתרון הכי טוב" |
| **Most-aware** | Ready to buy | Push the offer | "מבצע מסתיים / נותרו יחידות — הזמינו עכשיו" |

Most dropshipping cold traffic is **problem-aware** → lead with the problem in
their words, then the mechanism. Pick the headline strategy that fits the stage.
